# Interview Notes: Teams Recap Follow-Through

Participant: PM leading cross-functional launch reviews

Context: Uses Teams meetings, Copilot recap, OneNote/Loop, and Planner/Jira

Key notes:

- Copilot recap is useful for catching up and remembering what was discussed.
- The participant did not complain that the recap was empty or inaccurate.
- After the meeting, she still creates a separate OneNote or Loop checklist.
- She copies possible decisions and follow-ups out of the recap manually.
- She often needs to confirm whether a line was a decision, a suggestion, or an unresolved question.
- She wants to leave the meeting with clear owners, risks, and next steps.

Rough PM note:

Maybe the issue is not recap quality. Maybe the issue is turning recap into execution state.
